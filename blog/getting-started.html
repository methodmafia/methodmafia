Your First 24 Hours as a Member

New member guide — how to navigate the VIP channel  ·  September 2026

Welcome. You have just gotten access to a library that most people spend months trying to find pieces of across forums, YouTube comments and closed groups. Here is how to make the most of your first 24 hours.

Step 1 — Read the Pinned Message First

Every section of the channel has a pinned index post. Read it before diving in. It tells you how the library is organised, where to find the most popular methods, and what rules to follow (the main one: do not share your invite link).

Step 2 — Start With the AI & Tools Section

If you are new, start here. The AI section has the most immediately useful methods and the clearest step-by-step instructions. Five methods to try in your first session:

  1. ChatGPT Plus 1-Month Method — the most-used in the entire library. Try this first.
  2. Canva Pro Admin Panel Method — useful for almost any digital work. Straightforward to follow.
  3. Perplexity Pro 1-Year Method — excellent for research. Very reliable as of 2026.
  4. Free EDU Email Method — unlocks student discounts on GitHub, Notion, Figma and more.
  5. GitHub Student Developer Pack — follows naturally from the EDU method. Huge value.

Step 3 — Do One Method Per Day

The library is overwhelming if you try to do everything at once. Pick one method per day, follow the steps carefully, and move on. Within a week you will have cut several hundred dollars from your monthly software costs.

A lot of members go quiet for a day or two after joining because they are busy applying everything. That is the right approach.

Step 4 — Search Before You Ask

Telegram has a search function. Before posting a question, search the channel for the tool name. There is a good chance someone has already asked — and the answer is pinned or in the recent history.

If you do not find an answer, post your question in the group. Someone who has done it will usually reply within the hour.

Step 5 — Check New Posts Daily

Between 2 and 4 new resources are added every day. It is worth spending 5 minutes each morning scrolling to see what dropped. The most recent additions are always at the top of each section.

You can mute the channel if you find the notifications too frequent, but check in manually once a day so you do not miss something relevant to your work.

What to Do If a Method Does Not Work

Platforms update their systems. A method that worked last week may need a small adjustment this week. If something is not working:

  1. Re-read the instructions carefully — most failures are a missed step
  2. Search the channel for recent comments on that specific method
  3. Post in the group: "X method — step Y is not working for me, [describe what happens]"
  4. The team will either fix the method or confirm it is being updated

Broken methods are typically fixed within a few days. This is covered by your monthly fee — you are not left with dead links.
