Common mistakes when choosing a design tool

These are general. They are not a Canva setup guide.

  • Buying a team seat for one person. If nobody else will log in, a team plan is extra cost.
  • Paying for three apps that do the same poster. Pick the one job you do every week, then the one tool that exports it.
  • A yearly bill in week one. Use a short plan until you know you open the tool every week.
  • Ignoring the file. Before you pay anyone, check that you can export the format you actually send — PNG, PDF, or MP4 — and that you can still open the file later.
  • Judging from a screenshot. A pretty preview is not the same as a file you can edit tomorrow.
  • Mixing up two kinds of payment. An invoice from the tool company is not the same thing as a membership library that lists a method for that tool.

Canva publishes its own plans and its own rules. The current price is on Canva's pricing page. What an account is allowed to do is on Canva's terms of use. Read those pages yourself. This guide does not copy their prices or their clauses, because both change.
