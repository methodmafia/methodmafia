How Payment Works

The complete process from order to VIP access  ·  Updated September 2026

We do not publish our payment details publicly — this protects you and us from fraud. Everything is handled directly on Telegram after you place your order. Here is exactly how it works, step by step.

Step 1 — Fill the Order Form

Go to the main page and scroll down to the order form. Fill in your full name, email address and Telegram username. Choose a plan:

  • Entry ($30) — one-time fee that covers your first full month
  • Monthly ($15) — for renewals from the second month onwards

Select your preferred payment method and press Submit Order. You will see your Order ID on screen — write it down or screenshot it.

Step 2 — Message Us on Telegram

After submitting, a Telegram window opens automatically addressed to @MMHQ_Support. If it does not open, message us directly. Send your Order ID in the first message.

We will reply with the current payment details for whichever method you selected. Payment details rotate periodically — never use details from a previous order or from anyone other than @MMHQ_Support.

Step 3 — Make the Payment

Send the exact amount using the method we gave you. Take a clear screenshot of the completed transaction, making sure the amount and reference are visible.

Important: We never ask for your PIN, OTP or password. If anyone claims to be us and asks for these, they are a scammer.

Step 4 — Send Payment Proof

Reply on Telegram with:

  1. The payment screenshot
  2. Your transaction ID or reference number

Every payment is verified manually by our team. We do not use automated payment gateways — this is intentional and keeps costs low.

Step 5 — Receive Your VIP Invite

Once we verify your payment, we send you a private one-time invite link on Telegram. This link works once and is specific to you — do not share it.

Timeline: 30 minutes to 3 hours after you send your payment proof. We check messages regularly throughout the day and evening.

You can check your order at any time at order-status.html. Enter the Order ID you received when you submitted the form. If you lost your Order ID, message @MMHQ_Support with your email or Telegram username and we will find it.
